Welcome

SheComputes is an initiative focused on creating an empowering space for women-identifying theoretical and computational scientists. We host guest speakers, professional development, social events, and outreach activities.

To join our mailing list or request more information, please contact the organizers via the organization's contact channels.

Events & Initiatives

  • SheInspires — annual invited speaker series celebrating inspiring women in STEM.
  • WeCompute — hands-on outreach for high school students introducing computational chemistry.
  • Networking socials, workshops (public speaking, career panels), and community meetups.

Event flyers and detailed schedules are available upon request from the organizers.
