SheComputes

Mentorship, community, and networking for women-identifying computational scientists.

Welcome

SheComputes is an initiative based at NYU focused on creating an empowering space for women-identifying theoretical and computational scientists. We host guest speakers, professional development, social events, and outreach activities.

Sign up for the mailing list: Join our mailing list.

Events & Initiatives

  • SheInspires — annual invited speaker series celebrating inspiring women in STEM.
  • WeCompute — hands-on outreach for high school students introducing computational chemistry.
  • Networking socials, workshops (public speaking, career panels), and community meetups.

See the original site for event flyers and more details: hoffmanngroup.info/shecomputes.
